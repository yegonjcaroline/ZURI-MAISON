A little about us

A home is made in the details.

ZURI MAISON began with a simple idea: the things we live with every day should feel good to use, look good to keep, and be made with care.

Our point of view

Less, but more considered.

We are a Nakuru furniture and homewares brand shaped by the way people actually live. A Sunday lunch that runs late. Friends who stay for another cup of tea. A favourite chair that becomes everyone's favourite chair.

Those moments are at the centre of our work. We look for honest materials, useful shapes and warm, natural finishes. We want our pieces to settle into your home and become part of its story.

We work with makers and suppliers to build a collection that balances comfort, character and long-term usefulness.
